## Farlands Reforged 0.6.0

**FarMan** (off by default): the pitch-black figure from the Far Lands creepypastas haunts players who stay out there. Cave noises, footsteps, whispers in chat, torches on the ledges, then him watching from afar, then right behind you. He never hurts anyone. Turn him on with `/farlands farman on` or `enableFarMan = true`. Still server-side only.

**Choose where the Far Lands start**: `farlandsStartX` and `farlandsStartZ` (or `/farlands set x|z <distance>`) bring them closer on each axis, from 1 up to the classic 12,550,821, and the real terrain moves with them. Only newly generated chunks change. These replace `farlandsStartCoordinate`, which only moved the advancement.

With default settings, terrain is identical to 0.5.0.
